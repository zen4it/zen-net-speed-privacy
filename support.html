ZEN Net Speed — Support

Questions, bug reports or feature requests: email admin@zencomputers.us. We usually reply within two business days.

Speed results look low

Close other apps that are downloading, move closer to your router, and try another server from the server picker. Results over WiFi are limited by your WiFi link speed, which the app shows on the Network screen.

WiFi name or nearby networks are missing

Allow location access when asked. Android and iOS only let apps read WiFi network names when location permission is granted. Location is used on your device only.

"Server is busy" message

The speed test server is rate-limiting requests. Wait a minute or pick another server.
