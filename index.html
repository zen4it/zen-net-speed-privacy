ZEN Net Speed — Privacy Policy

ZEN Net Speed does not collect, store on any server, or share any personal data. There are no user accounts, advertising or analytics.

Network tests

To measure speed the app exchanges test data with public speed test servers (Ookla Speedtest.net servers or Cloudflare). To show your public IP address and internet provider, the app queries a public IP-lookup service (ipwho.is, ip-api.com or ipify.org). These services see your IP address as part of a normal internet connection, under their own privacy policies.

Location and WiFi

Android and iOS require location permission for apps to scan WiFi networks and read the connected network name. The app uses this only on your device to show nearby networks, signal and channels. Location is never stored or transmitted.

Local network

The device scanner and ping tools contact devices on your own network only when you start them.

Stored data

Speed test history is saved only on your device and can be deleted in the app or by uninstalling it.

Children

The app is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone.
