ZEN Net Speed — Privacy Policy

ZEN Net Speed has no user accounts and runs no servers or analytics of its own. We, the developer, do not collect or store your personal data. The app is free and shows ads from Google AdMob, which processes some data as described below.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app shows a banner ad and, after some completed speed tests, a full-screen ad. No ads are loaded while a speed test is running. Google AdMob may collect your device's advertising identifier, IP address (and an approximate location derived from it), ad interactions and diagnostic data, to deliver, measure and personalise ads and to prevent fraud. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland the app asks for consent through Google's consent form before personalised ads are shown; you can change your choice any time with the privacy icon on the History screen. On iPhone, personalised ads are shown only if you allow tracking in the App Tracking Transparency prompt (Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking). On Android you can reset or delete your advertising ID in the system settings.

Network tests

To measure speed the app exchanges test data with public speed test servers (Ookla Speedtest.net servers or Cloudflare). To show your public IP address and internet provider, the app queries a public IP-lookup service (ipwho.is, ip-api.com or ipify.org). These services see your IP address as part of a normal internet connection, under their own privacy policies.

Location and WiFi

Android and iOS require location permission for apps to scan WiFi networks and read the connected network name. The app uses this only on your device to show nearby networks, signal and channels; ZEN Net Speed never stores or transmits it.

Local network

The device scanner and ping tools contact devices on your own network only when you start them.

Stored data

Speed test history is saved only on your device and can be deleted in the app or by uninstalling it.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13.
